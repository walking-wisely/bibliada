Privacy Policy

Last updated August 6, 2026

Short version

Bibliada does not collect any data about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDK of any kind in the app.

What the app stores, and where

Bibliada saves your settings (such as which verse is currently shown, and your display preferences) locally on your Mac, in a storage container shared between the app and its widget. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us — we have no server and no way to receive it even if we wanted to.

The one network request the app makes

To display Bible verses, Bibliada fetches text from bible-api.com, a free public API, over HTTPS. Each request contains only a Bible verse reference (e.g. "John 3:16") — nothing that identifies you. As with any web request, your IP address is visible to bible-api.com's server as part of normal internet routing; that is standard server-side logging by that third party; it is not something we collect, receive, or have access to. If the network is unavailable, the app falls back to a small bundled catalog of verses and continues to work fully offline.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
